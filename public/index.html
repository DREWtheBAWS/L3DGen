trust the frontsearch all round

Keep the rotation search narrow. The shape model places your image at the front, and a rounded subject looks near-identical from every angle — measured on a low-poly creature the match varied by only 0.001 between opposite sides, so a wide search picks a side at random. Isometric art usually sits 20–45° above the horizon.

Blending unreached areas makes gradients, and the midpoint of two of your colours is a hue that is in none of your reference images. This pulls those areas back onto colours your art actually uses, then re-lights each one to the texel's own brightness — so shading and detail survive and only the wrong hue goes. Try 8–16 colours. Areas your art reaches are never altered.

This is what paints geometry your reference art never showed — a satchel or strap the shape model invented on a side you did not draw. The depth and normal maps come from the same orthographic camera the bake reprojects through, so the generated detail lands on the mesh exactly. Without the lock there is no good denoise setting: low only re-tints, high drifts off the silhouette.

Rigging

Fits the 25-joint mixamorig:* hierarchy to the finished mesh by finding its extremities along the surface, so it works from any A- or T-pose. Needs a roughly humanoid shape — two arms, two legs, a head. Apply Mixamo clips through a retargeter (Unity Humanoid, Blender, Godot): the rest pose here is your mesh, not Mixamo's. Check step7-rig-skeleton in the steps gallery before you rely on it.
